1. Unzip SenseAI_GitHub_Milestone_Starter.zip.
2. Open Terminal and go to the extracted folder, for example:
   cd ~/Downloads/SenseAI_GitHub_Milestone_Starter
3. Test the verified detector:
   python3 sense_ai_change_detector_v2.py --threshold-mm 100 --min-bins 3
4. Test the NEW experimental localization stage:
   python3 sense_ai_localize_v1.py --threshold-mm 100 --min-bins 3
5. Open README.md and figures/*.png; edit docs/experiments.md to match your records.
6. Once reviewed, initialize a Git repo and publish it to a new repository.
7. This is post-HackMIT RPLIDAR development, not a claim that SLAM/object recognition was completed at HackMIT.
